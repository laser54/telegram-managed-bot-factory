# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Give and accept feedback in good faith.
- Protect private data, credentials, and security-sensitive details.
- Focus disagreements on technical evidence and project goals.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, publishing private information without permission, or deliberately exposing credentials/security-sensitive data are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues, or participation that violate this policy.
